```text

```

Thus agent 1 is better off under global quota, agent 2 is indifferent, and agent 3
is better off under cap-and-trade. Both regimes have the same price, production,
aggregate consumption, total quota, and total emissions.

For every agent and regime, the result records:

- full consumption;
- own-electricity utility component;
- endowment, operating-dividend, quota, and total income;
- operating ownership income by firm;
- quota income by firm;
- tax rebate marked not applicable.

## Welfare language

The comparison is within-agent at fixed emissions and belongs to the constrained
Pareto context. It is not a full Pareto comparison across emissions. Example 2 does
not provide cardinal utility functions or a social welfare objective, so the report
does not fabricate aggregate/weighted welfare and does not compute an “optimal carbon
policy.”
